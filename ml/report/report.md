## Reading the numbers

- **Exact band** is the share of course grades whose band was predicted exactly; **within one band** allows a neighbouring band.
- **Grade-point error** and **SGPA error** are mean absolute errors on the 0-4 scale.
- **Early-warning AUC** ranks students by their chance of at least one R or F in the semester (0.5 is chance, 1.0 is perfect); **caught in top 25%** is the share of students who did get an R or F who were among the quarter flagged.

The proposal chose a decision tree (CART). A single tree proved unstable here, and its early warning ranked students worse than their own average does, so, following the proposal's improvement strategy, the deployed model is a random forest, which matches or edges the baselines. With grade history alone the student's own average is a strong baseline: the data holds one cohort, so no course had been taken before the semester it is tested on, and there are no mid-semester, CA or attendance marks yet. Those in-semester signals are the next features to add.
